Chaotic Command is operated by people who manage a Discord server. It is not currently a member self-service app, and ordinary members do not need to sign in to use its setup workflow.

## What you may see

After a server owner or administrator applies a template, you may see new roles, a category, and channels such as general discussion, introductions, announcements, or help. The exact items depend on the template they choose.

New roles are created with zero permissions and are not automatically assigned to members. Your server's administrators decide whether to configure those roles and assign them later.

## Channel access

New channels inherit category or server visibility and may be visible to members by default. The template does not make a channel private or decide which members should have access.

Follow your server's rules and ask your server administrators if you are unsure which channels you should use or what a new role means.

## Who to contact

Chaotic Command does not replace your server's moderation or support process. Contact your server owner or administrator for questions about role assignments, channel access, server rules, or changes made to the server.
